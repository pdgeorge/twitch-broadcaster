    Party guide

    A reminder of how the party game runs. The numbers come straight from the controller, so they stay right when you tune it.

    Channel-point rewards

    The controller goes by the reward's title, in any capitals.

    • … takes a seat in the party.
    • … adds a daily login, so more exp per message and more voices.
    • … adds a first login. Some counts hand out cosmetics: see Automatic rewards.

    Joining

    • There are … seats, and only the redeem fills them.
    • When the party is full or the character is dead, the bot says so in chat. Refund those redeems by hand.
    • Seats don't run out. A member stays until !kick, !newparty, death, or a controller restart.
    • Members get a card at the bottom right, and what they type is read aloud in their own TikTok voice (commands aren't). More voices unlock at … daily logins.

    Exp and levels

    Everyone who chats earns exp, not just the party: …, at most once every … seconds. A level-up raises max HP, refills HP and makes the Dabling bigger.

    LevelTotal expMax HPNew chatter, flat out

    Flat out means a message at least every … seconds.

    HP, death and revival

    • HP only moves when you move it, with !smite, !bless or !grant.
    • At 0 HP a character dies: out of the party, off the tavern floor, and unable to rejoin.
    • !revive <name> brings them back at full HP, … down. Add a number to change the cost: !revive <name> 0 is free.

    Good to know

    • A controller restart, which every deploy does, empties the party. Characters are saved, so members just redeem again.
    • Refreshing the browser source blanks the party cards until a member chats.
    • Chatters leave the tavern floor after … minutes without chatting.

    DM commands

    For mods and you. Names are Twitch logins, with or without the @.

    !grant <name> exp <n>Add exp. A negative number takes it away, and levels follow.
    !grant <name> hp <n>Change HP, up to max. 0 or less kills.
    !smite <name> <n>Deal n damage. 0 HP kills.
    !bless <name> <n>Heal n, up to max.
    !revive <name> [levels]Bring back the dead.
    !give <name> <n>Give gold. Nothing spends it yet.
    !give <name> <cosmetic>Give a cosmetic, worn straight away if that slot is empty.
    !take <name> <cosmetic>Take a cosmetic back.
    !kick <name>Send a member back to the tavern and free the seat.
    !newpartyEmpty the party.
    !sheet <name>Level, HP, exp and gold in the billboard spot for … seconds.

    Everyone's commands

    !rolld20 + level for your own character, or !roll <name> for someone else's. A 20 gets fanfare and confetti, a 1 a sad trombone. Rolls change nothing: you decide what they mean.
    !wardrobeWhat you own and what you're wearing.
    !equip <cosmetic>Wear something you own.
    !unequip <cosmetic or slot>Take it off.
    !soundsList the sound commands.
